Who We Are

An engineering partner built for Africa's networks.

WHAT WE DO

Anchor Telecoms is an engineering services company supporting the telecommunications industry across Nigeria and the African continent — delivering products and services of international standard, in partnership with leading operators, OEMs and infrastructure companies.

OUR HISTORY

Our roots trace back to 1998, as the telecommunications division of Anchor Business Systems Limited. With the introduction of GSM in Nigeria in 2001, we redirected our focus to this growth sector, and were incorporated as a private limited liability company in January 2005. Two decades on, we've grown into a Pan-African turnkey infrastructure provider.
